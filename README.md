## 🚀 Project Overview

**Rural ERides Go** is a production-grade React, Vite, and Capacitor mobile application engineered and heavily field-tested for personal electric vehicle (PEV) enthusiasts[cite: 8]. Designed to bypass closed-ecosystem mobile restrictions, this app delivers high-precision telemetry monitoring, live GPS breadcrumb routing, physics-based diagnostics, and a decentralized global community network[cite: 8].

This application officially launched in tandem with the **150+ Subscriber Milestone** on the Rural ERides digital media channel, celebrating a growing community of open-source tech enthusiasts and micro-mobility riders!

## ✨ Core App Features (v7.4.0)

Version 7.4.0 transforms the software into a full-scale PEV operating system:

* **Live GPS Breadcrumb Routing:** The telemetry engine secretly drops GPS waypoints every 0.05 miles. Broadcasting a run to the Community Board automatically generates an interactive, multi-waypoint Google Maps route of your exact path.
* **Predictive Maintenance Physics Engine:** The diagnostic dashboard tracks Tire Tread Life, Brake Pad Wear, and Drivetrain/Hub integrity based on your lifetime telemetry mileage[cite: 10].
* **Battery Health & Voltage Sag Logbook:** Calculates resting voltage vs. minimum sag under heavy throttle loads to detect cell degradation over time[cite: 10].
* **Atmospheric Physics & Weather Matrix:** Pulls 12-point live weather data (Solar UV Index, pressure, cloud cover, feels like, dew point, humidity, solar dawn/dusk, trail surface temperature) and dynamically adjusts Wh/Mile efficiency based on freezing temperature penalties and headwind/tailwind resistance[cite: 10].
* **Crash Detection Auto-SOS:** 10-second emergency countdown modal for severe impacts. If not canceled, it dispatches an emergency SMS with exact GPS coordinates[cite: 10].
* **Financial & Environmental Impact Engine:** Calculates total financial savings, gas saved, car wear avoided, CO2 reduced, and cent-per-mile operating costs against local fuel prices and car MPG benchmarks[cite: 10].
* **Rider Radar & Tac-Radio Comms:** Sub-meter precision tracking enabling multi-device fleet monitoring, Ghost Mode privacy toggle, custom Group Ride meetups, and a built-in secure voice/messaging Walkie-Talkie system.
* **Grounded AI Co-Pilot:** Powered by Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite with customizable operator personas (Companion, Mechanic, Systems) for mechanical inspections and local scouting.
* **Global Spec & Parts Engine:** Deep lookup database to audit live market inventories, compare multi-vehicle specifications side-by-side, and verify dynamic fastener torque ratings[cite: 10].
* **YouTube Media Console:** An integrated streaming deck supporting continuous loops, playback speed overrides, and personal timestamped video notes saved directly to your local vault[cite: 10].
* **Ironclad Legal Protocol:** A mandatory 7-point onboarding briefing handling liability, traffic laws, and high-voltage battery warnings with automatic routing straight to the Secure Pilot Registration portal.
* **Omnibus Settings Matrix:** Unprecedented UI customization including Glassmorphism intensity controls, dynamic island alerts, swipe navigation, background textures, and auto night-mode scheduling.

## 📦 Deployment & Installation

This project is fully stable and ready for field deployment. Performance telemetry, sensor polling, and radar synchronization are actively tuned during live field runs. 

* **Download:** Grab the latest signed `app-release.apk` binary directly from the [Releases Page](https://github.com/bradley3398/Rural-Erides-GO/releases).
* **Installation:** Transfer the APK file to your Android device or testing tablet. *Note: Ensure you enable "Install from Unknown Sources" in your device settings to bypass the Play Protect warning for this custom repository deployment.*
